Privacy

Privacy Statement

Clean software, clean website. We respect your privacy.

🔒 The Short Version

We do not track you, we do not show advertisements, we do not sell data, and both the website and desktop application operate locally on your machine.

1. Website Privacy

The official SA:GE website is built as a static site.

  • Zero Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics scripts, marketing pixels, or invasive telemetry.
  • Zero Advertising: There are no ads or promotional trackers anywhere on this website.
  • Zero Unnecessary Cookies: We do not set tracking cookies or store persistent profile cookies on your device.

2. Desktop Application Privacy

The SA:GE desktop application runs entirely on your local machine:

  • Local-First Storage: All map files, project manifests, and user preferences are stored strictly on your local disk (under your chosen project directory and your user AppData folder).
  • No Telemetry: SA:GE does not send your projects, file paths, or usage telemetry to remote servers.
  • Auto-Updater: When checking for updates, the application makes a single anonymous HTTP GET request to download the official update/manifest.json metadata file. No personally identifiable information or system identifiers are transmitted.

3. External Links & Services

Our website provides direct links to external platforms such as GitHub (for bug reports, releases, and discussions). When navigating to GitHub or external sites, their respective privacy policies apply.

4. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy statement or our open-source tools, please open an issue or start a discussion on our official GitHub repository.
