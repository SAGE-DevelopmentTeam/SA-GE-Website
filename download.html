Quick Setup Guide

1

Download & Extract

Download the official SAGE-v1.1.0-Portable.zip and extract the archive to your preferred location (e.g. C:\Tools\SAGE).

2

Run Instantly

Launch SA:GE.exe. Because it is self-contained, no installer or runtime setup is necessary.

3

Create & Test

Create or open a project in SA:GE, design your map, and playtest your custom map in Stardew Valley.
