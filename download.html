Quick Setup Guide

1

Download & Extract

Download the official release archive and extract the folder to your preferred location (e.g. C:\Tools\SAGE).

2

Launch & Configure

Launch SA:GE.exe. SA:GE automatically locates your Stardew Valley installation or lets you pick the directory.

3

Create & Playtest

Create a new project or open an existing Content Patcher mod, design your map, and click ▶ Playtest with SMAPI.
